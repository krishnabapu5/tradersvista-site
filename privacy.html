Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

TradersVista ("we", "us") publishes free forex and gold education on this website, on our Telegram channel (t.me/TradersVistaOfficial) and on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. This policy explains what information we handle and why. Contact: tradersvista.official@gmail.com.

This website

This is a static information site. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability, under its own privacy policy.

Telegram

When you join our Telegram channel, Telegram's own privacy policy applies. We see the total number of members, not a list we store or use. Polls in our channel are anonymous: we only see the total votes for each option. If you message our bot, Telegram delivers the message to us; we do not keep it or use it for marketing.

Instagram, Facebook and YouTube

When you follow, like or comment on our posts, the platform's privacy policy applies. Through the platforms' official APIs we read statistics about our own accounts only, such as follower counts, views, likes and comment counts, to see which content is useful. We do not collect or sell personal data about you.

YouTube API Services

TradersVista uses an internal tool, "TradersVista Publisher", that relies on YouTube API Services to upload our own educational videos to the TradersVista YouTube channel and to read that channel's basic details and statistics. It is used only by TradersVista, for the TradersVista channel, and does not access any other user's YouTube account or data.

  • By using our content on YouTube you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data YouTube processes.
  • Data accessed: the TradersVista channel's ID, title and statistics, and permission to upload videos to it.
  • Storage: the authorisation token is stored securely on our own server and is never shared or sold. Channel statistics are kept only as weekly totals for our internal growth report.
  • Revoking access: the channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, we delete the stored token, and any stored API data is deleted within 30 days.

Children

Our content is meant for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
